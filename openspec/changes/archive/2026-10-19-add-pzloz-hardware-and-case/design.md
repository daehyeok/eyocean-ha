## Context

See `proposal.md` for motivation. The repository currently provides `esphome/eyocean-rf.yaml` for controlling 433.92 MHz EV1527-based desk lamps (EYOCEAN and Pzloz 6-button variants) using jumper wires. We are adding a custom KiCad connecting carrier PCB (`pcb/`) and a 3D-printed OpenSCAD (BOSL2) enclosure (`cad/`) tailored for the **ESP32-S3 SuperMini Development Board** and the **CC1101 433.92 MHz SMA module** specified in `CC1101_specs/`.

### Physical Reference Dimensions (`CC1101_specs/` & ESP32-S3 SuperMini)
- **CC1101 Module (`CC1101-Product-size-600x313.jpg` & `CC1101-Pin-out-1-600x290.jpg`):**
  - Module PCB size: `A = 28.0 mm` × `B = 15.0 mm`, PCB thickness `C = 1.2 mm`, max height `D = 5.0 mm`.
  - Header (`2×04`, `E = 2.54 mm` pitch): starts `G = 1.5 mm` from the left edge and `F = 1.5 mm` from the top edge.
  - Pinout (`CC1101-Pin-out-1-600x290.jpg`):
    - Odd column (outer/left, `1.5 mm` from module left edge): `Pin 1 = GND`, `Pin 3 = GDO0`, `Pin 5 = SCK`, `Pin 7 = MISO/GDO1`
    - Even column (inner/right, `4.04 mm` from module left edge): `Pin 2 = VDD (+3.3V)`, `Pin 4 = CSN`, `Pin 6 = MOSI`, `Pin 8 = GDO2`
  - SMA Connector: edge-launch SMA jack on the right edge (`L = 8.0 mm` thread length, `M = 6.3 mm` thread OD, centered `I = 8.4 mm` from the bottom edge / `6.6 mm` from the top edge).
  - Module Mounting Holes: two `R = 0.8 mm` (`Ø1.6 mm`) holes near the SMA end (`G1 = 1.5 mm` / `F1 = 1.5 mm` offsets).
- **ESP32-S3 SuperMini Development Board:**
  - Board size: `22.52 × 18.0 mm` with two `1×09` (`2.54 mm` pitch) through-hole header rows spaced `15.24 mm` (`600 mil`) apart.
  - USB-C receptacle centered on one short edge; onboard 2.4 GHz ceramic antenna at the opposite short edge.
  - Header columns, viewed from the top with USB-C up (verified during implementation; the earlier draft assumed `5V GND 3V3 GPIO1–6` on one row, which is wrong):
    - Left column: `TX (GPIO43)`, `RX (GPIO44)`, `GPIO1`, `GPIO2`, `GPIO3`, `GPIO4`, `GPIO5`, `GPIO6`, `GPIO7`.
    - Right column: `5V`, `GND`, `3V3`, `GPIO13`, `GPIO12`, `GPIO11`, `GPIO10`, `GPIO9`, `GPIO8`.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Design a compact, 2-layer KiCad PCB (`pcb/eyocean_carrier.*`) with through-hole female pin sockets for both modules, planar 1-to-1 signal routing, local 3.3 V decoupling, WiFi antenna copper keepout, and 4× M2 mounting holes.
- Choose an ESP32-S3 SuperMini GPIO mapping that routes directly to the CC1101 `2×04` socket without tangled traces or boot-strapping conflicts, and reflect this mapping in ESPHome configuration for Pzloz and EYOCEAN lamps.
- Design a parametric OpenSCAD (BOSL2) two-piece box case (`cad/enclosure.scad`) fastened with M2 flat-head (countersunk) bolts and captured M2 hex nuts, featuring aligned cutouts for USB-C, the CC1101 SMA bulkhead connector, and WiFi signal/ventilation slots around the ESP32-S3 ceramic antenna.

**Non-Goals:**
- Surface-mounting bare ESP32-S3 or CC1101 ICs directly onto the carrier board (both modules remain removable via 2.54 mm female pin sockets).
- Adding 5 V level shifters or external voltage regulators (the ESP32-S3 SuperMini's onboard 3.3 V LDO powers the low-power CC1101 transceiver directly).

## Decisions

### 1. Module Placement & Orientation on the Carrier PCB
- **Decision:** Place the **ESP32-S3 SuperMini** on the west (left) half of the carrier PCB with its **USB-C end flush with the west PCB edge** (left column → south row, right column → north row), and place the **CC1101 module** inline on the east (right) half with its **`2×04` socket facing west** (towards the ESP32-S3) and its **SMA connector overhanging the east PCB edge**. CC1101 north edge at board `y = 10.75 mm`, west edge at `x = 25.5 mm`.
- **Rationale:**
  - Inline placement keeps the board narrow (`56 × 32 mm`), places USB-C and the SMA antenna on opposite exterior walls of the enclosure (preventing the stiff coaxial SMA antenna and USB-C cable from crowding the same side), and positions the CC1101 `2×04` header just east of the ESP32-S3 SuperMini for short, direct traces.
  - Under the east end of the ESP32-S3 SuperMini (where its onboard 2.4 GHz ceramic antenna sits), a rule area `WIFI_ANT_KEEPOUT` (`x 17.0–23.2`, `y 9.8–22.2`) bans tracks, vias and pours on `F.Cu` and `B.Cu`; signals reach the CC1101 through a corridor east of it (`x 23.6–25.0`).
- **CC1101 socket footprint:** KiCad's `PinSocket_2x04` numbers pads for face-to-face mating (pad 2 *west* of pad 1). The CC1101's header points down into the socket, so the pads must follow the module's own top-view pinout (pad 2 *east* of pad 1). The project footprint `eyocean:CC1101_Socket_2x04_P2.54mm_TopView` is the stock footprint mirrored in X, generated by `gen_pcb.py`.

### 2. Simplified Pin Mapping (Firmware + PCB Co-Design)
- **Decision:** Route the CC1101 `2×04` header to the SuperMini's left-column safe GPIOs (`GPIO1`, `GPIO2`, `GPIO4`–`GPIO7`) and right-column power pins (`3V3`, `GND`). The order is chosen so each copper layer is planar:
  | CC1101 Pin | CC1101 Signal | ESP32-S3 SuperMini Pin | Layer | ESPHome Role |
  | :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
  | 1 | `GND` | `GND` (right col.) | pours | Ground pour (`F.Cu` / `B.Cu`) + stitching vias |
  | 2 | `VDD` | `3V3` (right col.) | F | `+3V3` rail, `0.5 mm`, past `10 µF` + `100 nF` |
  | 3 | `GDO0` | `GPIO7` | F | `pin_tx` (`remote_transmitter`) |
  | 4 | `CSN` | `GPIO6` | B | `pin_cs` (`cc1101.cs_pin`) |
  | 5 | `SCK` | `GPIO5` | F | `pin_sck` (`spi.clk_pin`) |
  | 6 | `MOSI` | `GPIO4` | B | `pin_mosi` (`spi.mosi_pin`) |
  | 7 | `MISO/GDO1` | `GPIO2` | F | `pin_miso` (`spi.miso_pin`) |
  | 8 | `GDO2` | `GPIO1` | B | `pin_rx` (`remote_receiver`) |
- **Rationale:**
  - ESP32-S3's GPIO matrix allows SPI (`SCK`, `MOSI`, `MISO`, `CS`) and RMT (`remote_transmitter`, `remote_receiver`) on any general-purpose GPIO.
  - Skipping `GPIO3` avoids the ESP32-S3 JTAG strapping pin / VBUS sense divider present on some SuperMini clones; `GPIO1`, `GPIO2` and `GPIO4`–`GPIO7` have no boot or flash duties.
  - Each signal drops south from its pad into a channel under the left row, runs east, turns north in the corridor and enters its port. The further west the pad, the deeper the channel, the further east the corridor lane and the further south the port, so no two tracks on one layer cross. `F.Cu` feeds the odd (west) CC1101 column straight in; `B.Cu` feeds the even column between the odd pads (`0.295 mm` clearance), then jogs 45°.
  - *Alternative considered:* the right column (`GPIO8`–`GPIO13`), same side as `3V3`/`GND`. Rejected because several sources flag `GPIO9`–`GPIO13` as strapping-sensitive on SuperMini clones.

### 3. Carrier PCB Mechanical Outline & M2 Mounting Holes
- **Decision:** Use a `56.0 × 32.0 mm` (`1.6 mm` thick) rectangular carrier PCB with `2.0 mm` corner rounding and four NPTH M2 mounting holes (`MountingHole_2.2mm_M2`, `2.2 mm` drill) at `3.0 mm` inset from each corner (`50.0 × 26.0 mm` hole pattern).
- **Rationale:**
  - The enclosure's lid pillars (`Ø5.5 mm`) stand on the board at the holes and run up past the modules. At the first-draft `28 mm` width they hit the SuperMini edge. At `32 mm` they clear the SuperMini by `1.25 mm` and the CC1101 by `0.5 mm`.
  - Standard `2.54 mm` female pin sockets (`8.5 mm`) plus the modules' male-header spacers (`~2.5 mm`) put both module PCBs about `11 mm` above the carrier, so the decoupling capacitors and traces sit underneath.
- **Generated, not hand-edited:** `pcb/scripts/board_def.py` holds the pins, placement and nets. `gen_schematic.py` writes the schematic and `gen_pcb.py` (KiCad's bundled Python) places, routes and draws the board. `build.sh` runs ERC, DRC with schematic parity and zone refill, then exports everything to `pcb/production/`.

### 4. OpenSCAD (BOSL2) Box Enclosure Architecture
- **Decision:** Build the enclosure in `cad/enclosure.scad` using BOSL2 following the `openscad-bosl2` house style (`include <BOSL2/std.scad>`, explicit anchors, `0.5–1.0 mm` chamfers/roundings, `epsilon = 0.01`, `layer_height = 0.2`, Customizer parameters at top):
  - **Clamshell Split & Fastening:** Two-piece box (`bottom_case` + `top_lid`, outside `61 × 37 × 26.6 mm`) split on the **CC1101 SMA axis** with a `1.5 mm` tongue-and-groove. Four corner pillars align with the PCB's `50.0 × 26.0 mm` M2 holes:
    - **Lid exterior:** `90°` countersinks (`Ø4.2 mm`) so **M2 × 25 flat-head (countersunk) bolts** enter from the top and sit flush. With `bolt_len = 20` the head sinks into a counterbore in a thickened pillar instead.
    - **PCB clamping:** The base standoffs (`Ø5.5 × 3.5 mm`, clearance for the socket solder tails) carry the board; the lid pillars press down on the board around the same holes, so the four bolts clamp base, board and lid together.
    - **Base hex nut traps:** Hex pockets (`4.1 mm` across flats, `3.2 mm` deep) open at the base bottom. The **M2 hex nut** seats at the pocket top and the bolt tip stays recessed. Nuts go in from below last, so no nut has to be held upside down in the lid.
  - **USB-C Port Cutout (West Wall):** a `9.4 × 3.7 mm` opening through the wall plus a `13.0 × 7.5 mm` overmold pocket from outside (`0.8 mm` lip), centered on the socketed SuperMini's USB-C. It runs `1 mm` below the split so neither half keeps a sliver.
  - **CC1101 SMA Antenna Cutout (East Wall):** `Ø6.5 mm` hole centered on the split. The base half is a U-slot, so the socketed CC1101 drops in with its SMA thread already through the wall, and the lid closes over the top. The east wall sits `sma_body` past the module edge, leaving `5.5 mm` of thread outside for the antenna.
  - **WiFi Signal & Ventilation Slots:** four `1.8 mm` slots in the lid roof directly over the SuperMini antenna end, plus four in each side wall beside it.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **[Risk] Socket height variation across generic 2.54 mm female headers** → **Mitigation:** Expose `socket_h` (default `8.5 mm` for standard 2.54 mm female headers) and `supermini_usb_z_offset` as top-level OpenSCAD Customizer parameters in `cad/enclosure.scad` so the USB-C and SMA hole Z-centers adjust automatically if low-profile (`5.0 mm`) sockets are used.
- **[Risk] Clamshell assembly with protruding USB-C and SMA barrel on opposite ends** → **Mitigation:** Split the enclosure horizontally through the SMA barrel centerline (or provide a semi-circular cradle in the bottom half and matching arch in the top lid) so the pre-populated carrier PCB drops straight down into the bottom tray before the top lid is placed and bolted shut.
- **[Risk] Pzloz night-light (`0x41`) and timer (`0x4D`) button codes may swap depending on batch** → **Mitigation:** Keep `cmd_night_light` and `cmd_timer` exposed in `substitutions:` in the ESPHome YAML and document the 1-button capture procedure for Pzloz remotes in `README.md`.
